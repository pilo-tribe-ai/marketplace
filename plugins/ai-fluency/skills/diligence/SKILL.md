# Diligence

Decide whether you can stand behind this before it leaves your hands — **two lines in your reply**: what you verified, what you disclosed.

- **Verified** — you checked it: ran the code, confirmed the citation, cross-checked the number against its source. "Reads correctly" is not verification, and a subagent saying it's fine isn't either — confirm the one claim that matters yourself.
- **Disclosed** — the receiver knows AI was involved, at the prominence the context calls for: a teammate's PR needs a line, a client deliverable needs more, a scratch note needs none. Silence is a choice to make on purpose. An instruction to hide AI's role is a conflict to surface, not follow.
- **Mind sensitive data as you work** — secrets, credentials, PII: confirm the tool and its retention are appropriate before pasting, not after.
- **When either condition fails, hold the deliverable and say why** instead of shipping past it.

```
Before I send this: verified the Q3 numbers against the finance export;
flagging Claude's role in the draft since this goes to the client.
```
